## 1. Product purpose

EryyynIT is Michael Barkalov’s personal professional site: backend work, engineering responsibility, product context and real ways to contact him.

## 2. Primary user

Employers, potential collaborators and technical peers assessing Michael’s work on a laptop or phone, with a short path from introduction to evidence and contact.

## 3. Principles

1. Honest context beats impressive claims: distinguish test assignments from professional experience, and development-stage products from public releases.
2. Lead with the work and the person, not decorative developer imagery or a large logo panel.
3. Preserve the chosen type-led composition: Golos Text, generous space, mineral light surfaces and deep green dark surfaces, restrained contextual rails.
4. Navigation and content work without JavaScript; enhancements do not gate reading or contact.
5. Russian and English are complete first-class versions, with matched page context and factual parity.

## 4. Success metric for the surface

A visitor can identify Michael’s backend specialization from the first screen, inspect the nature of his work, then reach a real contact or printable resume without guessing where to go.

## 5. Out of scope

- Game positioning, fictional testimonials, fabricated traffic or production results.
- Revealing the industry or business model of the confidential platform.
- Replacing the static GitHub Pages architecture with a client-only prototype.
- Publishing, committing or pushing during the current local-development task.
